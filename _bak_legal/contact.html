What to write to us about

Something in a review is wrong

This is the one we care most about. If a price is stale, a feature has moved, or we got a fact wrong, tell us and we will check it and correct the page. Being accurate is the entire point of this site, so corrections jump the queue.

Ask us to test a tool

Using something we have not covered? Send the name and what you use it for. We cannot promise a full review, but requests with real detail get taken seriously.

Business, partnerships, or sponsorship

Say what you have in mind. We will tell you straight whether it is a fit. Paid placements do not change our verdicts, and we do not write positive reviews in exchange for money, so please do not ask.

Anything else

A question about how we test, a suggestion, or something you think we are missing. All of it is welcome.

What to include

  • The name of the tool, if your message is about one.
  • What you were trying to do, and what actually happened.
  • A link to the page, if you are correcting something we published.

The more specific you are, the faster we can help.

What we cannot help with

We do not run the tools we review, so we cannot reset your password, refund your subscription, or fix a bug you hit. For anything about billing, accounts, or technical faults, contact that tool's own support team. They can help; we cannot.

Privacy

Your email address is used to reply to you and nothing else. We do not add you to a list, and we do not pass it on. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
