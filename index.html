About

Why this site exists

I'm a content creator, and writing the weekly report at my day job used to be my biggest headache. I spent every day wrestling with words. Before AI, a single article could take hours, sometimes days. AI shrank that dramatically, but leaning on it alone brings its own problems.

So I publish my real, hands-on experience with the AI copywriting tools I use: what each one does well, where it trips you up, what it costs, what the alternatives are, and how they stack up against each other.
