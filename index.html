There is no shortage of prompts that promise to strip the AI out of your writing. We run each one through several models and show which pairings actually survive a real read.

Why this site exists

I'm a content creator, and writing the weekly report at my day job used to be my biggest headache. Every day is spent wrestling with words. Before AI, an article could take hours or even days. AI shrank that dramatically, but leaning on it alone brings its own problems.

So I'm publishing my real, hands-on experience with the AI copywriting tools I use on this site: the details, the pitfalls, the cost, what alternatives exist, and how each stacks up against the others.
